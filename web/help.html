Help

Short answers to common questions.

What does each page do?

Dashboard: start a scan and see a summary of your inbox.

Pending Actions: the suggestions from your last scan. "Newsletters to unsubscribe from" sends an unsubscribe request to that sender. "Clutter to move to Trash" moves a group of similar emails to Gmail's Trash. Nothing happens until you approve items and press "Do approved actions".

Stats: who sends you the most mail, and a download of your data as CSV files.

Run History: every scan and every time actions were carried out.

Accounts: add another Gmail account, switch between them, or sign out.

Settings: how far back to scan, AI sorting, the daily summary email, and senders to never touch.

How do automatic scans work?

Turn them on under Settings → Automatic scans and pick a time and days. GmailCleaner keeps running in the system tray (near the clock) after you close the browser tab, and scans every signed-in account at that time. You get a notification when it's done.

Scans only run while GmailCleaner is open. If your computer was off at the scheduled time, GmailCleaner scans once when it next starts (if it's been less than a day). To have it open automatically, turn on "Start GmailCleaner when I log in".

Automatic scans only make suggestions, unless you've turned off "Ask me before doing anything" and listed senders under Automatic actions. To stop them for a while, choose Pause automatic scans in the tray menu.

How do I undo a cleanup?

GmailCleaner never deletes email permanently. It moves it to Gmail's Trash, which keeps it for 30 days.

To get an email back: open Gmail, click Trash in the left menu (under "More"), select the emails and click Move to Inbox. Unsubscribing can't be undone from GmailCleaner; if you change your mind, sign up with that sender again.

Why does Google say "Google hasn't verified this app"?

Getting Google's verification costs thousands of dollars a year, and GmailCleaner is a free, open-source project. The warning doesn't mean anything is wrong.

To continue, click Advanced, then Go to GmailCleaner (unsafe), then allow the permissions. GmailCleaner runs only on your computer: your email is never sent to the GmailCleaner authors or any server of theirs.

The shared sign-in can only be used by 100 people in total. If sign-in stops working because of that, you can create your own free sign-in client and upload it under Settings → Google sign-in.

How do I get a free Gemini key for AI sorting?

1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.

2. Click Create API key and copy it.

3. In GmailCleaner, go to Settings → AI sorting, paste the key, click Test key, then Save key.

The free tier has a daily limit. If it runs out, AI sorting is skipped for the rest of the day and everything else keeps working.

Privacy

Everything GmailCleaner knows is stored on this computer. There's no account with us, no tracking and no analytics.

GmailCleaner only talks to: Google (to read and tidy your Gmail), Google's Gemini AI if you turn on AI sorting (it sends the sender, subject and a short preview, never the full email), and the unsubscribe links of senders you approve.

Your Gemini key is kept in your computer's password manager. Your Google sign-in is saved in a private file that only your user account can read.

Something isn't working

"Please sign in again": Google ended the sign-in (for example after a password change). Go to Accounts and click "Sign in again".

"A scan is already running": wait for it to finish. Only one scan per account runs at a time.
